Quick guide for a first-time trader

LONG means buying because you expect the price to rise. SHORT means selling to profit from a fall; availability and rules depend on your market and broker.

  • Uptrend: look for LONG setups after support holds or a breakout is confirmed.
  • Downtrend: avoid fresh buys; consider SHORT only after a confirmed breakdown and if permitted.
  • Mixed trend: WAIT. Not trading is a valid decision.
  • Before every trade: choose entry, stop-loss, target, and affordable position size. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.

Method notes: trend is prioritized over oscillator extremes; volume is used as confirmation; three-month return provides a basic momentum ranking; benchmark-relative strength is not yet part of this standalone build. Fibonacci levels, pivot levels, ATR and indicator values are descriptive calculations, not forecasts. Point & Figure, Elliott Wave counts, cycle dating, market breadth and full intermarket analysis are not inferred reliably from a single daily OHLCV series and are not represented as confirmed signals here.

Detection is rule-based and uses price history only. The fit score shows how closely a chart matches the textbook shape and is not a probability of success. Targets are the classic height projections. On pure random price noise this scanner still flags an active pattern on roughly one chart in four, so treat a pattern as one input among several.
